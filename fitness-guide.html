Getting Started with Fitness

Fitness has been something I've gotten really into over the past year. The biggest thing I've learned is that consistency matters way more than intensity. A lot of people burn out because they go too hard too fast. Starting slow and building up over time is how you actually make progress. Recovery is just as important as the workout itself — sleep, hydration, and rest days are not optional. If you want to learn more about training the right way, the NSCA resource library has a ton of good evidence-based articles.
